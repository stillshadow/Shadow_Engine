# TA-029 Visualize and rotate scene lights

## Scope

- draw persistent viewport markers for Directional, Point, and Area lights;
- show Point Light range and Area Light shape/direction;
- allow Area Light rotation through the existing `E` rotate Gizmo while keeping `W` for translation;
- preserve the existing scene data and renderer light path.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All scene lights have a visible viewport representation when they are in front of the camera.
2. Point Light shows a center marker and approximate range outline.
3. Area Light shows a rectangle and emission direction marker.
4. Area Light `E` rotation updates its emission direction; Directional Light has no position Gizmo.
5. Debug build, CTest, and `git diff --check` pass.
